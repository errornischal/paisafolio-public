What this does and does not count

Each row is (what you sold it for − what it is worth now) × how many. Green means the price fell after you sold, so selling saved you that much. Red means it rose, and that is profit you would have had by holding.

Three things it cannot know, so read it as one input and not a verdict:

It does not know what you did with the money. Selling at what looks like a loss here was still the right call if you put the proceeds somewhere that did better.

It does not subtract broker commission, DP charges or capital gains tax, so what actually reached your account was less than the sale price.

It does not adjust for bonus shares, rights or splits. If a holding went through one after you sold, one unit no longer means what it meant then, and that row is not comparable.
